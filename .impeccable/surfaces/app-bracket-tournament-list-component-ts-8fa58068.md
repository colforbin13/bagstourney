# Home page (`/`)

Scope: the public landing content at the top of `/`, plus the tournament list beneath it. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a first-time organizer deciding whether this is simple enough to trust with their event. Action: create a free account. Proof: the blind draw itself, working on the page. No testimonials, logos or usage numbers exist; none may be shown.

Constraints: prerendered with no API data; signed-out HTML is hydrated, then signed-in buttons swap in. Existing copy is kept. Wrong outcomes named by the user: corporate software, childish or jokey, dark and moody. Must stay spare and unintimidating.

The same colours and fonts roll through every other screen by token; app screens stay on white stock and use the sheet colour sparingly.
